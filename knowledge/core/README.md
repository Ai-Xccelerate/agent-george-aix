# Agent George — Knowledge Base

Source material for designing and building Agent George. Synthesized from CSM walkthroughs of the current Onyx onboarding and partner-management process.

## Files

1. [`01-csm-onboarding-process.md`](./01-csm-onboarding-process.md) — How onboarding works today, the artifacts involved (NDA, contract, order form, ValueCase Onboarding Hub, Support Hub), and the ongoing role of the CSM. No customer names — generic process reference.
2. [`02-agent-george-role.md`](./02-agent-george-role.md) — What Agent George is, who its users are, what it owns vs. doesn't own, the interaction model, integrations, and guardrails.
3. [`03-agent-george-lifecycle-steps.md`](./03-agent-george-lifecycle-steps.md) — Concrete steps Agent George executes across the three lifecycle phases: Onboarding, Health & Cadence, and Success Sprint / Renewal Health.

## Conventions

- All process references are generic (no specific partner or customer names).
- Vocabulary: **Partner** = MSP, **Partner Admin** = primary administrator at the partner, **End Customer** = the partner's customer, **Support Hub** = the Onyx product, **Onboarding Hub** = the ValueCase space used to track onboarding tasks.
- Two customer journeys: **Journey A** (Partner Onboarding) and **Journey B** (End Customer Onboarding by the partner).
